Read these before acting on the counts

Each was found while building the underlying report. None is blocking, all are worth an owner.

Fitted means listed

A vehicle counts as fitted when its make, model and series resolve to a fitment row that has tyre sizes against it. That says the sizes exist in the catalogue, not that the customer was shown them. tyre_options is empty on every mirrored row, so the log cannot confirm what was actually served.

Empty tyre options

tyre_options is the literal string [] on all 324,350 mirrored rows. 1,140 rows carry a real tyre_options_added_at timestamp anyway, which says the field was populated upstream and lost in the mirror.

Vehicles with no make

4,506 vehicles carry 29,540 requests, averaging 6.6 each against about 1.0 everywhere else. Every spec field is null, so they can be neither classified nor fitment-checked. They come from the AU_SCA iframe integration, dormant since 8 Dec 2025.

Bulk load in mid-2026

May and June 2026 hold 123,590 vehicles between them, 38% of the whole log, against a normal 5,000–7,000 a month. Almost all are unfitted, which drags every whole-log rate down. Confirm what that load was before quoting the headline.

Model names fragment

Counts group on the raw model string, so variants split into separate lines. Ford Falcon holds 4,161 vehicles under FALCON and 8 more under seven spellings including two of “FALCON GL,S-PAK” differing by one space.

Unstable vehicle id

The fitment table’s vehicle_id is documented as session-ephemeral. Of 50,811 ids that match the log, 99.6% agree on make and model but the year rarely does, so the id resolves to the wrong variant. All joins here use make, model and series instead.

Reversed timestamps

updated_at falls before created_at on 313,070 of 324,350 rows. Dates here take the later of the two, but per-request timing cannot be trusted until this is explained.

Stale fitment snapshot

The fitment table is a single snapshot dated 16 Jun 2026, three months behind the log. Vehicles first seen after that date can count as unmatched for no other reason.
